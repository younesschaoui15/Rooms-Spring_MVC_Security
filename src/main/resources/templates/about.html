About

Rooms

A role-based collaboration platform where every application role — Admin, Manager, Team Lead, and others — gets a dedicated space to communicate, coordinate work, and stay aligned on what matters to them.

Why Rooms?

Purpose-built spaces instead of scattered tools

In many organizations, different roles operate in silos. Admins configure the system, managers oversee teams, and individual contributors focus on execution — but they rarely share a single, purpose-built space for their work.

Rooms solves this by giving each role its own Room: a private, focused environment where members of that role can collaborate without noise from unrelated channels. Built with Spring MVC, Spring Security, and Thymeleaf, Rooms keeps conversations, tasks, and discussions organized in one place instead of scattered across email, chat, and spreadsheets.

What you can do in a Room

Every Room is tailored to the responsibilities of its role

Communicate

Connect with peers who share the same responsibilities and keep conversations in context.

Manage tasks

Create, assign, track, and complete tasks relevant to your scope of work within each Room.

Discuss topics

Start organized discussion threads grouped by subject, with replies and mentions that stay on topic.

Roles & permissions

Access to each Room is controlled by role — you only see what you're authorized to enter

Privileged roles

ADMIN · SUPER_ADMIN · MODERATOR

  • View all rooms across the platform
  • Create and manage rooms (update, configure, remove)
  • Manage topics and replies in every room

Standard roles

PROJECT_MANAGER · DEVELOPER · TESTER · others

  • Create topics only in rooms dedicated to their own role
  • Reply only to topics posted by users who share the same role
  • Focused discussions within their dedicated space
